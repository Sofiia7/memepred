FlipTheMeme brings short UP or DOWN rounds on meme coins to Robinhood Chain.

For people who enjoy outcome betting, this is a way to call a meme coin’s next move without buying or shorting it.

This is a testnet prototype. The demo pools use simulated prices, and every stake uses test ETH.

First, connect the browser wallet. This recording uses two founder wallets to demonstrate both sides of one round.

The first wallet stakes 0.005 ETH on UP. One transaction is enough: the contract wraps ETH internally, so there is no separate swap or approval.

The second wallet takes DOWN with another 0.005 ETH. Both sides fund the round; the protocol does not need a house bankroll.

Confirm the DOWN transaction in the wallet.

A new betting round opens every five minutes. An active round finishes sixteen minutes after opening, rather than immediately after the click.

The result compares a future strike average with the exit price. Movement before that strike does not decide the outcome.

In this recorded round, DOWN won. The winning 0.005 ETH stake can collect 0.0098 ETH: a 1.96 times payout.

Press Collect and confirm the transaction to receive the payout in native ETH.

The position now shows Collected: 0.0098 ETH. This is a real testnet transaction, shown in the live browser recording.

FlipTheMeme is a working, unaudited testnet prototype. Try the rounds, inspect the verified contract, and explore the source code.
